# Monthly Summary — 2026-10

## Overview

First month of Q4, three workstreams. The **home** refresh rework: the 30s beat and its manual click now
refresh the whole page (line statuses + Today feed + Last Week first page) instead of the lines alone,
the refresh control moved on top of the links section on mobile, and its transient "Updated"
confirmation was fixed so it actually fires — and only on a clean, click-armed refresh. Paired with
it, the backend now excludes today from the Last Week window by default (the frontend sends no new
variable, so either deploy order is correct). The **console/spotting** selection UX: the select-mode
switch is gone, rows are selectable by clicking anywhere on them, and Shift-click range-selects. And the
**link conversation tree**: the related-link grouping of the previous wave becomes an ordered, nested,
editable tree, with its "N links" count moved inside the first link's own card. And the console's
**links queue** moves to `/console/links` (the old URL redirects, and both nav lists followed it),
wraps its long URLs and titles instead of clipping them, and now **retires the row when you approve
it** — publishing and completing in one ordered, single-reload sequence. And the last two **home**
interaction fixes of the day, both found by clicking the real thing in a browser: the refresh
control now says **Updating** while its own request is in flight before the transient **Updated**, and
its trigger shrink-wraps to the spinner + label instead of an invisible full-width strip; and the
quick submit box's "Enter a URL" note is **submit-gated** (blur stays quiet), which needed a
`novalidate` on the `<form>` because signal-forms reflects the schema's `required` onto the DOM and
the native bubble was aborting submission before the handler could run. And the day's last **home**
fix closes the loop those two opened: returning to `/` used to **delete** the refresh control for the
rest of the session, because the route injector retains `HomeStore` across visits and `start()` was
re-applying the `null` that `stop()` had just paused the beat with. The month's first day of **console
accessibility to its own features** followed: the links tree's "Nest under…" had reused the untargeted
grouping minimum of two ticks even though a targeted nest is a real write with one, and the console's
reorder actions were unreachable from the default Pending queue — both fixed with a shared `canNest`
rule and a one-click "Show all links". The day closes on a small **ads polish**: the "Advertisement"
disclosure caption reads "ADVERTISEMENT ⓘ" with the glyph on the right, padded into a pill, and the
popover's hover hotspot shrank from the whole caption row to that pill alone.
