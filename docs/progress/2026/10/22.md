## [2026-10-22] insiden: the Last 12 months chart reads the backend aggregate

- F213 slice 1: the "Last 12 months" chart takes its counts from the backend and no longer counts in the browser (`9e03de9`). The answer is at most three rows a month. The old path fetched every incident of two calendar years, ongoing ones included.
- F213 slice 1: the client places each row's count and does nothing else (`bucketSeverityCountsByMonth`). It zero-fills the 12-month axis, ignores a row outside it (a full `YYYY-MM` match), sums repeated rows and counts `OTHERS` and unknown severities into the "not shown" note.
- F213 slice 1, the window: `historyDateBounds` runs from 1 January of the first month's year to 31 December of the last month's year. The bounds come only from the route-derived selected month, never from the clock. Both sit on month edges, because a bound inside a month would give that month a partial count (backend `1e72161`). A `dateBounds` computed with value equality feeds the request, so paging inside the same pair of calendar years re-buckets the loaded rows and sends no new request. The read still waits until the section is opened.

- F213 slice 1, the document: `startDate` and `endDate` stay `Date!`, and `groupBy: MONTH` stays in the document.
